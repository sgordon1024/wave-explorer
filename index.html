About this animation

The full spectrum spans trillions of times in frequency. To keep the wave readable, its displayed frequency is compressed into 1–5 cycles across the view. Crests move at a constant display speed: more cycles means faster oscillation and shorter spacing. Space and time scales change between settings; this is not a literal cross-band scale comparison. Real vacuum speed is always 299,792,458 m/s. Field scales normalized: heights are independently normalized (E = cB), and colors identify fields, not actual light color.

IN EVERYDAY LIFE

THREE THINGS TO TAKE WITH YOU

One wave. Many ways to meet matter.

01 / THE RELATIONSHIP

More wiggles. Less space.

Frequency counts oscillations each second. Wavelength measures their spacing. In a vacuum, f × λ = c. Double the frequency and the wavelength halves. The wave doesn’t travel faster.

02 / THE INTERACTION

The material matters.

There is no universal “penetration ranking.” Transmission, absorption and reflection depend on frequency, material and thickness. Glass can transmit visible light while absorbing much infrared; metal can reflect radio waves.

03 / THE ENERGY

Brightness isn’t frequency.

Each photon carries energy E = hf. A stronger classical wave carries more energy overall, but raising its amplitude doesn’t change energy per photon. Your eyes detect only a tiny slice of this spectrum.
